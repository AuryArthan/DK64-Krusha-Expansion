# Krusha Expansion v0.16.0

Five colored keys are hidden on DK Isles. Open the tiny offshore prison and give the sixth spot in the tag barrel to a Kremling with a bazooka and a voice loud enough to clear a room. His campaign adds 350 silver bananas, 63 silver coins and 24 Golden Bananas. There’s a surprise waiting at 111%.

Apply **DK64-TagAnywhere+Krusha.bps** to a clean, big-endian Donkey Kong 64 (USA) ROM (SHA-1 `CF806FF2603640A748FCA5026DED28802F1F4A50`). Start a fresh Adventure file. For the key hunt, leave Krusha enabled and select Tag-Anywhere OFF or ON. Speed Mode grants his rescue immediately.
