Play as Krusha from the start of Donkey Kong 64. This edition builds on Isotarge’s Tag Anywhere. Tap the D-pad to bring Krusha into the action, or find his face between Chunky and Donkey in the tag barrel.

Shoulder his bazooka, lift a boulder, or take him into a battle arena to earn a crown. Once the Kongs unlock Crystal Coconuts, Krusha can spend one on a shout powerful enough to clear nearby enemies. He can also use Bananaports and photograph Banana Fairies with the shared camera. The five original Kongs collect the colored bananas, coins, blueprints and Golden Bananas.

Turn on Speed Mode for a quick start, or follow the original Kongs’ usual unlock progression. The Widescreen option adjusts the HUD and menus for 16:9, and the Krusha option lets you switch back to the five-Kong roster.

Apply **DK64-TagAnywhere+Krusha.bps** to a clean, big-endian **Donkey Kong 64 (USA)** ROM (SHA-1 `CF806FF2603640A748FCA5026DED28802F1F4A50`). [Emulator setup and screenshots for v0.6.1](https://github.com/AuryArthan/DK64-Krusha-Expansion/tree/v0.6.1#playing)

For the five-key rescue and Krusha’s own silver collectibles, play [Krusha Expansion v1.0.0](https://github.com/AuryArthan/DK64-Krusha-Expansion/releases/tag/v1.0.0).
